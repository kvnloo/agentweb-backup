## ENG-673: the poll no longer treats a bounce from our own sending side as the contact's (2026-10-30)

Found by checking the poll against two real Smartlead campaigns: Smartlead's `is_bounced` contact list also holds contacts whose bounce was `sender_bounce` (our sending mailbox was refused or is broken, which says nothing about the contact's address): 41 of 57 on one campaign and 2 of 9 on the other, every one of them also in Smartlead's separate `is_sender_bounced` list. Reading the bounce text cannot tell the two apart (the real messages are all `5xx`, and 37 of 60 carry `5.7.x` policy and reputation codes). Before this change the poll would have blocked those contacts as bounced and the withdrawal sweep would then have unsubscribed them in Smartlead for good. Now a contact counts as bounced only when it is in the bounced list and NOT in the sender-side list; the sender-side list is read once per pass, only if a bounce needs it; if it cannot be read in full (a failed read, or more contacts than the pages read), no bounce is acted on for that campaign that pass, and it is logged once a day. Opt-outs and replies are unaffected. On the two real campaigns the poll would now act on 7 of 9 and 16 of 57. The poll was live in production but could not have acted on a real contact: the only production runs are finished tests on deleted campaigns. A contact whose emails bounced from both sides is not blocked (the safe direction; the webhook still delivers a genuine bounce). Bounce text is not read at all, so no per-contact message reads were added; a temporary (`4xx`) bounce still cannot be told from a permanent one by the poll, but none was seen in 60 real messages and the webhook classifies by text.
