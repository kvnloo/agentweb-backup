## ENG-2743: daily oncall flag for scheduled runs whose cost jumps (2026-11-12)

New cron `backend/src/cron/scheduleCostJumpCron.ts` (registered in `server.ts`, behind `isCronEnabled()`), read-only. It ticks every 15 minutes (and at boot) but only acts during 07:00-07:59 UTC (`SCHEDULE_COST_JUMP_SEND_HOUR_UTC`), once per day per process, so a deploy outside that hour never repeats a day's email. It reads the usage ledger (`usageRecordDb.scanByDateRange`, the last complete UTC day plus the 7 days before it) and sums each schedule's agent-loop cost per day (feature `agent_task:scheduled:<scheduleId>`, all models together). A schedule is flagged when that day cost at least $3 and at least 2x the median of its billed days in the previous 7 (needs at least 3 billed days; new schedules are skipped). All flagged schedules go out as ONE email a day to the oncall list (`oncall_emails` setting, or `ONCALL_EMAIL_LIST`) through the new `emailService.sendOncallEmailOnly`: email only, no Linear issue (Manan's choice, 2026-11-12; `sendOncallAlert` files a Bug/Needs-PM issue for Harsha since 2026-11-07). Production only. A failed read or send is tried again on the next tick of the hour. Only a restart inside that hour, after the email went out, can repeat it; with several instances each would send (prod runs one).

Backtest on the prod ledger (Oct 25 - Nov 11, nothing sent): flags Nuerraa's newsletter `b2bce0d0` on Oct 26/28/29 (3.8-5.7x), `04b34124` on Nov 1 ($33.60, 7.4x, Opus calls) and Oct 26/28, plus two smaller jumps (Oct 29, Nov 3); nothing Nov 4-11. A gradual climb that never doubles in one day (hourly `0052c195`, $5.6 -> $11.6 over five days) is not flagged.

No new setting, table or write. Thresholds are constants in the module. The per-run cap itself stays with ENG-2812.
