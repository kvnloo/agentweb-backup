## ENG-2987: enrichment estimate calibration check and paid-email-search measurement (2026-11-16)

Follow-up to the estimate recalibration (#4038, ENG-2720). Adds a read-only check, `scripts/enrichment-estimate-calibration.py`, and its runbook, `docs/enrichment-estimate-calibration-runbook.md`: join prod enrichment run logs to the usage ledger per account and day and compare booked dollars per contact with the estimate's quote. `--since` is required and must be the day after the last pricing or pipeline change (a window that reaches back before a change shows a false DRIFT: a 14-day window on 2026-11-16 did, ratio 2.61); keep the same `--since` until the data is enough, and run it at least every 14 days because the log group keeps 14 days. Internal accounts (platform admins and anyone on the `agentweb.pro` and `argusoft.com` domains or their subdomains) are left out, and the retune gate needs 100 contacts from at least 3 accounts that each supplied 10 or more. With internal accounts excluded the 2026-11-04 to 2026-11-13 window has only two customers, so the check is still INSUFFICIENT DATA and nothing was retuned.

Measured on 2026-11-16: one customer-fired paid Apollo email search since 2026-11-04 (a Nuerraa user on 2026-11-05, one request in the 2026-11-13 recalibration sample); its placeholder was stamped `completed` with a verified email 2.8 seconds after it was created, which is consistent with a real found-email callback (we cannot prove who called the webhook and have not seen the payload). 0 of 8 real people given as name and company only fired the search; the 5-person run booked $0.42 (about $0.08 a lead). Decision recorded in the runbook: no cache-aware quote (the cache keys need a domain only known at run time, and asking Apollo costs a paid lookup).
