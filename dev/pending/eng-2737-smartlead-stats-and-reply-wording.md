## ENG-2737: Smartlead campaign stats for Emma, and plain wording for Smartlead replies on every channel (2026-11-04)

- **New Emma tool `get_smartlead_campaign_stats`** (read only, `emma/smartleadPushTools.ts`). Input: `campaignId` (from `list_smartlead_campaigns`), optional `accountId`. Output: contacts emailed, emails sent (follow-ups included), replies, bounces, unsubscribes and the lead total, from Smartlead's `GET /campaigns/{id}/analytics` (`getCampaignAnalytics` in `smartleadService.ts`, through the hardened `smartleadRequest`), plus the note "Smartlead's counts can lag a few minutes behind a recent push or send." It goes through `SmartleadPushService.campaignStats`, so it uses the caller's own or org-shared connection exactly like `list_smartlead_campaigns`, follows the same `SMARTLEAD_PUSH_ENABLED` switch, and writes nothing to Smartlead or the CRM. Smartlead sends the counters as strings; a missing or garbled counter is an error, never a zero. Opens and clicks are left out on purpose: connector campaigns do not turn tracking on, so they would read zero. Offered in chats and blocked for share-link visitors like the other Smartlead tools; `prepareTurnContext.golden.json` is re-recorded for that one tool name.
- **Smartlead replies read as a sentence on Slack and WhatsApp.** Only when `isSmartleadReplyAlert` is true (the test the email uses). Slack: the header and fallback text are `A new lead "Name" replied to your campaign` and `... List: <list name>` via a new optional `headline` on `LeadNotificationPayload` (fallback text and the Name, Email, Company, Phone and List values escaped for Slack, because a contact's details are not ours to trust as markup; the fields, the "Received at" line and the Open CRM button are unchanged; the compact format ignores it as before). WhatsApp: the template variables are the contact's name (one safe line, cut at 40 characters) and `replied to your campaign on <list>. <CRM link>`; the template itself is unchanged. The name, the address fallback and the 40 character cut come from one helper (`smartleadReplyHeadline` / `smartleadReplyContactLabel`) that the email subject now uses too, so the three can not drift apart. Every other event, and an `email.received` that did not come through Smartlead, sends byte for byte what it sent before (pinned in `leadListAlertService.smartleadReplyChannels.test.ts` and `slackLeadNotification.test.ts`). The reply text itself stays email only.
- **Email caption reworded** to `The contact's own words. Check any link before you click it.` (the longer "with the earlier thread left out" was dropped: the quote cutter only knows English, French, Spanish, German and Italian headers, so for other languages that sentence would be untrue).
- No table, index, IAM or environment change.
