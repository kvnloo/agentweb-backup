## ENG-673: a contact marked do-not-contact is refused on every send path (2026-10-30)

Build 3 of slice 3. Smartlead bounces and unsubscribes now set the do-not-contact status automatically, so every way AgentWeb itself can message a contact has to refuse one. An audit of all send paths found these open, now closed: the one-off `send-email` and `reply-email` routes and the CRM LinkedIn message and invite routes (409 `do_not_contact`, no override, not even the Smartlead confirmation); Emma's generic `email` tool and `POST /api/emma/email` and `send_email_from_sender` (address-only sends: the address is looked up and any copy of the contact in any list counts when its owner is the sender, the mailbox owner or anyone in the sender's org, because an opt-out is the contact's and holds for the whole team; another customer's block never counts; a lead with no owner recorded is resolved through its list; a lookup that fails or hits its row cap refuses the send instead of letting it through); Emma's LinkedIn invite/message/InMail and X DM (by the named lead, and for LinkedIn also by profile URL); and the drip worker, which now checks the lead it just read before every send regardless of the sequence's exit settings (a sequence saved with `onUnsubscribe` off, or an older one, used to skip the check) and exits an enrollment whose lead no longer exists. New sequences always store `onUnsubscribe: true`. Already covered before: group email, forward-lead auto-send, Emma `send_email_to_lead`, the Smartlead push. No schema change.

Known limits: the lead-based checks (CRM routes, drip, `send_email_to_lead`) look at the one lead record in front of them, so a person unsubscribed in one list but active in another is only stopped on the address-based paths; plus-addressing (`a+x@b.com`) is not matched to `a@b.com`; sends typed to an address with no lead behind it (test sends, the client-report recipient) are not checked on purpose; a LinkedIn profile is matched by exact URL with or without a trailing slash (a differently written URL for the same person is not found); an X DM is checked by lead or address only, there is no lookup by handle; WhatsApp and voice calls were not traced.
