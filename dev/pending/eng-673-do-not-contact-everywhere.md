## ENG-673: a contact marked do-not-contact is refused on every send path (2026-10-30)

Build 3 of slice 3. Smartlead bounces and unsubscribes now set the do-not-contact status automatically, so every way AgentWeb itself can message a contact has to refuse one. An audit of all send paths found these open, now closed: the one-off `send-email` and `reply-email` routes and the CRM LinkedIn message and invite routes (409 `do_not_contact`, no override, not even the Smartlead confirmation); Emma's generic `email` tool and `POST /api/emma/email` and `send_email_from_sender` (address-only sends: the address is looked up among the sender's own leads, any copy of the contact in any list counts, another customer's block does not; a failed lookup refuses rather than sends); Emma's LinkedIn invite/message/InMail and X DM (by the named lead, and for LinkedIn also by profile URL); and the drip worker, which now checks the lead it just read before every send regardless of the sequence's exit settings (a sequence saved with `onUnsubscribe` off, or an older one, used to skip the check) and exits an enrollment whose lead no longer exists. New sequences always store `onUnsubscribe: true`. Already covered before: group email, forward-lead auto-send, Emma `send_email_to_lead`, the Smartlead push. No schema change.

Known limits: sends typed to an address with no lead behind it (test sends, the client-report recipient) are not checked on purpose; a LinkedIn profile is matched by exact URL with or without a trailing slash (a differently written URL for the same person is not found); an X DM is checked by lead or address only, there is no lookup by handle; WhatsApp and voice calls were not traced.
