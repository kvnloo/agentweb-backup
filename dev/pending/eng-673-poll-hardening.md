## ENG-673: hardening of the Smartlead event poll after its final review (2026-10-30)

Follow-up to the hourly poll (#3848). A contact whose handling failed (a database error while blocking an opt-out) is no longer remembered as handled, so it is retried on the next pass instead of after six hours; a contact that matched nobody in the CRM is looked at again after thirty minutes (a lagging email lookup), one that matched after six hours. A status filter that Smartlead ignored or renamed would list the whole campaign as bounced or unsubscribed, so a filtered answer of 20 or more contacts that is as big as the campaign (read once per pass) is refused and logged. An opt-out is believed only when Smartlead's own flag on the contact is set, however it was found. A time from Smartlead later than our own clock is recorded as now, so it cannot out-date a CRM user's Resubscribe. A bounce already on file no longer hides a later opt-out from the poll (or the other way round): only an event of the same kind counts as already shown. Not changed: every polled bounce is still treated as hard (owner to decide whether to read the bounce text first);  Verified on a real account (2026-10-30): Smartlead's lead is ONE lead across the account's campaigns, so unsubscribing it in one campaign (or account-wide, as our withdrawal sweep does) shows it unsubscribed and blocked in every other campaign too. The poll therefore skips an opt-out or bounce for any marker whose Smartlead lead id is withdrawn on any marker of any lead record with the same address, so a withdrawal in one campaign or list is never read back as news in another.
