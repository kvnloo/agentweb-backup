## ENG-673: Smartlead reply alert email, with the contact's own words (2026-11-03)

A reply that comes back through Smartlead (`email.received` whose event data has `source: "smartlead"`) now sends its own plain-language email (`smartleadReplyAlertEmail.ts`) instead of the shared "Lead list alert: email.received" one. The branch is an early return in `leadListAlertService.sendAlertEmail`; every other alert type and source keeps the generic template exactly as before. Same recipients as before (the list owner plus `alertCcEmails`). The copy was approved by the owner (Manan) on 2026-11-03.

- Subject `A new lead "Name" replied to your campaign` (name in straight quotes, cut at 40 characters; the address when there is no name; no quotes when neither). Details panel (campaign, lead list, subject, received time in UTC), a "Their reply" block, one button, and a footer saying why the owner got it. HTML is table based with inline CSS, with a plain-text twin.
- Reply text comes from the webhook payload (`reply_message.text`, else `preview_text`; the real payload was captured on 2026-11-03). `replyExcerpt` in `smartleadEvents.ts` reads at most the first 10,000 characters, cuts the quoted thread (`>` lines, "On ... wrote:" in five languages, "Original/Forwarded message" rules, long underscore rules, an Outlook `From:` header block), keeps at most 40 lines and 1000 characters. The email shows at most 15 lines and 600 characters.
- The reply text rides only on the in-memory event to the alert. It is not stored (the engagement `data` never has `replyText`), it is not in Slack or WhatsApp alerts, and a reply found by the poll has none (the email then says Smartlead keeps the full reply).
- A contact controls their name, subject and words, so: everything is HTML-escaped; zero-width, bidi-override and control characters and U+2028/2029 are removed (`smartleadSafeText.ts`); the inbox preview line is fixed wording; the quote bar is grey (not the button blue) and a caption says AgentWeb has not checked the text or its links; the button only takes http(s) URLs. An earlier version of the excerpt cutter was quadratic on a long run of spaces (14 s on 100k spaces, found in review); it is now linear and tested.
- Known gaps, left on purpose: the Slack and WhatsApp alert for a Smartlead reply still shows the raw event name; the list settings screen still lists raw event names; the quote cutter is heuristic (a reply that itself starts with `>` lines is cut early and shows less, which fails safe; cutting is silent).
- No table, index, IAM or environment change.
