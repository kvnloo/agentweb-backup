## ENG-2818: stop setting three dead env vars on the backend EB environment (2026-11-26)

`backend/.ebextensions/99-enigma-eng2070.config` no longer sets `OPENROUTER_GATEWAY_TASK_CLASSES`, `DISCORD_INVITE_URL` or `GCS_BUCKET`. Nothing in `backend/` reads them (they were emptied on Sep 22 for the 4,096-char EnvironmentVariables limit, but the empty names still cost 65 chars). After this deploys, remove the three names from prod-3 with `update-environment --options-to-remove` (EB does not drop settings an older .ebextensions applied). Prod-3 then goes from 86 vars / 4,047 chars to 83 / 3,982, i.e. 49 to 114 chars of room for the launch flags (`ZOMA_BILLING_ENABLED`, `META_TOOLKIT_SPEND_CEILING`, a Databricks launch flag). Rollback: re-add the three lines (empty values) and redeploy; no behaviour depends on them.
