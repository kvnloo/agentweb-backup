# Submission checklist

- Strategy, at most six pages, with three observations and keep/change/stop decisions.
- Reproducible calculations, explicit denominators, and separate real/synthetic conclusions.
- Campaign/ad-set/ad plan, proposed budget and measurement plan with three prioritized tests.
- One finished 1080×1350 static ad and one 15–30 second 1080×1920 video, with editable files or reproduction steps.
- Preserved v1, Emma critique, decision log and revised final version after the hypothetical budget/qualification update.
- One substantive strategy improvement, one creative improvement and one rejected/corrected AI suggestion.
- Private walkthrough or annotated written equivalent.
- Sources/tools/asset-rights log identifying what data each tool received.
- Any product failures documented with reproduction steps and expected/actual behavior; no credentials.
